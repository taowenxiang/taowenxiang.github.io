# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is research supervisors, internship and full-time recruiters, and technical peers evaluating Wenxiang Tao's work. Course instructors and collaborators may also use the site to inspect working project outcomes rather than static claims.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Wenxiang's education, research, engineering experience, and selected projects with enough evidence for visitors to understand both the problems addressed and the systems built. Success means a visitor can quickly establish credibility, inspect a real project, and find a clear path to contact or collaborate.

## Brand Personality

Credible, clear, and curious. The voice is technically precise without becoming cold, confident without inflated claims, and willing to show how projects work instead of relying on labels.

## Anti-references

- Generic resume templates that reduce every experience to an identical card.
- Loud AI product marketing pages built from gradients, glass panels, vague claims, and excessive motion.
- Dense engineering dashboards that make a personal portfolio feel like an internal tool.
- Decorative interactions that obscure project evidence or slow down basic navigation.

## Design Principles

1. Show working evidence: link claims to live demos, source, publications, or concrete outcomes.
2. Make the visitor's first minute useful: identity, focus areas, and strongest work should be immediately legible.
3. Let technical depth unfold progressively: concise summaries lead to details rather than competing with them.
4. Preserve trust around AI demos: state data flow, credential handling, costs, and limitations before asking for access.
5. Keep personality in the composition and writing, not in ornamental UI effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and form feedback. Support reduced motion, avoid color-only status communication, keep touch targets usable on mobile, and ensure project demos remain understandable with assistive technology.
